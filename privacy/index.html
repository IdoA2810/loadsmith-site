Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · applies to the Loadsmith test version

Loadsmith is a lifting log for iPhone and Android, made by Ido Albeck in Israel. This page says what the app stores, where, who else sees it, and how you delete it. It is written to be read, not skimmed, and it describes what the app actually does today. When the app changes, this page changes with it, and the date above moves.

The short version. Your training log is yours. It is backed up to your account so it survives a lost phone. No advertising, no analytics, no selling or renting of data, ever. The only company that sees what you write is the one that has to in order to do the job you asked for: the database that stores the backup, the AI model when you ask it to write a program, the crash reporter if you leave it on, and the email service that sends your sign-in code.

Who is responsible

Ido Albeck, Israel, is the controller of this data. Write to support@loadsmith.app for anything on this page.

What the app stores

Your account

Your email address, which is how you sign in and how a sign-in code or password reset reaches you. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they also pass along the name on that account; the app does not show or use it, and it sits only in the sign-in record. Passwords are stored hashed by the authentication service, never readable.

About you

Whatever you give the app so it can plan sensibly: your date of birth or age, sex, height, bodyweight entries, and which units and effort scale you prefer. All of it is optional, and the app works without it.

Your training

Programs, training days, planned and logged sets: exercise, weight, reps, effort, rest, and any notes you type on a set, exercise or workout. Also what the app suggested mid-workout and whether you took it.

What you tell the program builder

When you talk to the AI program builder it remembers facts you tell it (“left shoulder clicks on overhead work”, “train four days a week”) so you do not have to repeat them. These facts can include injuries and other health information; the app treats them as sensitive data. You can read and delete each one under Settings → What the coach remembers. The conversation itself is kept only on your phone.

Bug reports

What you write, the screenshot you choose to attach (never one taken for you), the app version, the platform and the screen you were on. The screenshot is stored with the report, so attach one only if you are happy for the developer to see what is on it.

Crash reports (optional)

If Send crash reports is on in Settings, a crash sends the error, the screen it happened on, the app version and your account id. Never your workouts, your notes, or what you typed. The switch is honoured immediately.

Records the app keeps about itself

Which version of the in-app notice you agreed to and when; and for each AI request, the model used and the number of tokens it consumed, so the cost can be tracked. Not the content of the request.

What the app does not collect

No advertising identifier, no analytics or tracking SDK, no location, no contacts, no photos beyond the one you attach to a report, no microphone, no camera. The only device information recorded is the platform and OS version alongside a bug report or consent record.

Apple Health and Health Connect

Only if you connect it, and each part has its own switch in Settings:

  • Read bodyweight reads your weight entries. Nothing else in Apple Health or Health Connect is requested.
  • Send bodyweight writes the weights you type into Loadsmith.
  • Send workouts writes each finished workout as a strength-training session with its start and end time. No sets, weights or reps.

A weight read from Apple Health or Health Connect goes into your training log like any other weight, is backed up to your account with the rest of your log, and is shown only to you. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used to build a profile of you, and never passed to the AI program builder: only a weight you typed yourself can be. It is stored by the database provider below as part of your backup and goes nowhere else. Turn a switch off and nothing more is read or written; what was already written stays in the health app until you delete it there, and what was read stays in your log until you delete it here. You can also revoke the app's access from inside Apple Health or Health Connect.

Where it is stored and how it moves

The backup lives in Singapore (Supabase on AWS). The AI model and the email service are in the United States, the crash reporter in the European Union. If you are in the EU or UK, that means your data leaves your region; each of these providers offers standard contractual clauses or an equivalent for the transfer. Everything travels encrypted in transit, and the sign-in session on your phone is kept in the device's secure store.

How long it is kept

  • Your log, profile, remembered facts and bug reports: until you delete them in the app, or until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports: 90 days at Sentry.
  • AI requests at Anthropic: up to 30 days, then gone.
  • The bug-report email in the developer's mailbox: until the report is dealt with, and no longer than 12 months.
  • Nothing is kept after account deletion except the deletion request itself, for up to 90 days, as the record that it was done.

Deleting and taking your data with you

In the app you can delete any workout, set, note, bodyweight or remembered fact, and sign out at any time. To remove the account and everything in it, follow Delete your account: during testing this is a request by email, completed within 30 days, and it will become a button in Settings. To receive a copy of your data, email support@loadsmith.app from the address you signed in with; an in-app export is planned.

Your rights

Under Israel's Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, you may inspect the information held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, the GDPR additionally gives you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before, and the right to complain to your supervisory authority. For all of these, write to support@loadsmith.app; requests are answered within 30 days.

For EU and UK readers, the legal bases are: performance of the contract with you (your account and backup), your consent (the AI program builder, Apple Health / Health Connect, crash reports, each of which you switch on yourself and can switch off), and the developer's legitimate interest in keeping the app working (bug reports you send, the cost record of AI requests).

Age

Loadsmith is for people aged 16 and over. It does not knowingly keep an account for anyone younger; if you believe one exists, write in and it will be deleted.

Changes

This page is the current version; earlier versions are in the public history of its repository. A change that matters to you – a new recipient of your data, a new kind of data – is shown in the app before it takes effect, and you are asked to agree again.
